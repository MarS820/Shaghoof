Core Problem — Learning the Wrong 
Thing
 65% of students are disengaged by 8th grade. The core problem is not intelligence; it’s mismatch. 
Classrooms are optimized for a fictional "average" student and rely heavily on text and lecture. 
Students who learn visually or kinesthetically are left behind and internalize failure as a personal 
deficit. Generic AI tutors compound the issue by supplying answers rather than building cognitive 
skills—removing productive struggle that drives learning. 
Context: Daily breakdowns occur when material is delivered in a non-matching modality.
Root cause: One-size-fits-all pedagogy and assessment.
Emotional impact: Frustration →  shame →  learned helplessness →  disengagement.
[USERS] school students (ages 6–18), particularly those labeled "average" or "underachievers," 
including undiagnosed or unsupported students with Dyslexia, ADHD traits, or ESL 
backgrounds, who are falling through the cracks of one-size-fits-all education.
[NEED] Need to understand how they learn best across multiple dimensions (cognitive style + 
neurodiversity), build the resilience to learn in any environment, and receive content that is 
accessible, bias-free, and adapted to their specific processing needs—so they can retain 
knowledge, rebuild confidence, and reignite their passion for learning.
[SURPRISING INSIGHTS]
IQ predicts only 20% of academic success—passion and cognitive adaptability predict 
the other 80%.
1.
65% of students are disengaged by 8th grade—not due to low intelligence, but because the 
system doesn't speak their cognitive or neurological language.
2.
Generic AI tutors (ChatGPT) are worsening the crisis—students using them show declining 
exam performance because they outsource thinking instead of building neural pathways.
3.
Dyslexic, ADHD, and ESL students are disproportionately harmed by text-heavy, rigid 
platforms. Yet, when content is delivered in their accessible modality (e.g., audio for 
dyslexics, micro-interactive for ADHD, bilingual for ESL), their performance matches or 
exceeds neurotypical peers.

Solution Overview — specialized educational needs
Onboarding & Cognitive Profiling
Neuro-Adaptive VARK Quiz
5 puzzles that detect Visual, Auditory, Read/Write, and 
Kinesthetic preferences, plus optional teacher/parent 
checklist flags for Dyslexia, ADHD, and ESL needs.
Digital Twin Generation
Stores the VARK vector, SEN flags (Dyslexia, ADHD, 
ESL), and accessibility preferences.
Brain Wheel Dashboard
Animated donut chart with dynamically resizing slices 
and a center badge that upgrades from Bronze to 
Gold.
Dyslexia Suite
OpenDyslexic Font Toggle
Global font change for easier reading.
Universal Text-to-Speech (TTS)
"🔊 Listen" button on all text.
Spelling-Forgiving Grading
Pass 1 (Correctness) ignores spelling errors for flagged dyslexic students.
Voice-to-Text Input
Students can answer via microphone; the system assesses meaning, not spelling.
Bullet-Point Reformatting
Guardian Agent auto-converts paragraphs into lists and short sentences.
Line Focus Mode
Reading ruler highlights one line at a time.
ADHD Suite
Micro-Quest Mode
Lessons are broken into 2-to-3-minute quests with visible countdown rings.
Hyper-Interactivity
Tap-to-Reveal cards and drag-drop sliders replace scroll-to-read experiences.
Pulse Nudge (Tutor Intervention)
If the student pauses for more than 4 seconds, the Tutor prompts: "Let's keep the 
momentum!"
White Noise Toggle
Optional background audio such as rain or a coffee shop can be enabled in 
settings.
XP Rain
Constant +5 XP bursts after every micro-step.
Zen Mode
Full-screen, distraction-free reading environment.
ESL Suite
Bilingual Toggle
Instant Arabic/English, or other languages, side-by-
side translation.
Lexile Simplification
Guardian Agent auto-downgrades text to CEFR B1 
level.
Click-to-Define Glossary
Underlined complex words open a definition, image, 
and pronunciation.
Cultural Adaptation
Swaps Western idioms, such as "break a leg," for local 
equivalents.
Audio Speed Control
Slows TTS/audio to 0.75x without pitch distortion.
ASD (Autism Spectrum Disorder)
Predictable Routine Indicator
A visual timer or progress bar showing exactly how long each Quest will take and 
what comes next. Reduces anxiety from uncertainty. Micro-Quest Mode 
enhancement.
Concrete Over Abstract
Guardian Agent auto-converts metaphors and idioms into literal language (e.g., 
"The ball is in your court" →  "It is your turn to decide"). This is different from ESL 
cultural swapping—it's about literal comprehension. Guardian Agent enhancement.
Sensory Load Indicator
A small icon (🌿) that shows the current "stimulation level" of the lesson. Students 
can click it to reduce animations, sounds, or colors. Settings + Zen Mode 
enhancement.
Social Narrative Integration
Before starting a group activity or peer-review task, the system provides a short 
"social story" explaining the expected social script. Tutor Agent enhancement (for 
collaborative features).
Special Interest Personalization
If a student has a specific interest (e.g., trains, space, dinosaurs), the Creator 
Agent incorporates that theme into lessons. Creator Agent enhancement.

General Features 
SLCN (Speech, Language, Communication Needs)
Augmentative Communication Support
Symbol-based communication (e.g., PECS-style icons) alongside text for students with 
severe language delays. Guardian Agent generates a symbol strip for key concepts.
Simplified Question Stems
All quiz questions are auto-rewritten to remove complex syntax (e.g., 
"What is the vertex?" →  "Where is the turning point?"). Creator Agent + 
Guardian Agent collaborate.
Visual Scene Displays
Contextual images that show the entire scenario (e.g., a 
picture of a math word problem's setting) rather than

Psychology & Retention Mechanics
 Shaghoof is grounded in evidence-based learning science: VARK for modality discovery, Bjork’s 
desirable difficulties for resilience, spaced repetition and interleaving to combat forgetting, and 
generative processing to deepen encoding. Engagement levers use the Zeigarnik and Endowed 
Progress effects—small, ethical nudges that increase return and completion without gamified 
distraction.
